Before you book

Quick answers to what people ask most.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking form above: service, date, time. It opens in WhatsApp with your details filled in, and we confirm your slot there.

Do you do bridal and party hair?

Yes. Bridal and party styling is booked in advance, and a trial session is available before the day.

How long does a keratin treatment take?

Usually two to four hours, depending on length and thickness. We'll tell you the exact time when you confirm.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp at least a day before and we will move or cancel your slot.
